FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your trial.

How does the free trial work?
Every new account gets a 14-day free trial with full Professional features — no credit card required. When the trial ends you can pick a plan or drop to a paused account; your content stays saved either way.
What devices can I use as screens?
Almost anything with a screen: Android TV boxes, Amazon Fire TV sticks, Raspberry Pi, Windows PCs, BrightSign players, LG webOS and Samsung Tizen TVs — or just a web browser tab. Install the player app, pair with a code, and you're live.
Do my screens keep playing if the internet goes down?
Yes. Players cache their content locally and keep playing through an internet outage, then sync automatically once the connection returns. No blank screens.
Can I show live data like prices or dashboards?
Yes. Connect a Google Sheet or any REST API and place values on your slides as live tags — when the data changes, every screen follows. Great for menu boards, KPI dashboards and schedules.
How is pricing calculated?
Plans are priced per month and include a set number of screens — 3 on Starter, 15 on Professional. Need more? Move up a plan or talk to us about Enterprise volume pricing for large fleets.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel from your account settings in a couple of clicks. Your screens keep running until the end of the billing period.
